## 5. Core Platform Section

**Headline:** One catalog. Every channel. Always accurate.

**Intro line:** Kernle AI is where your product data lives, gets enriched, and gets sent out — instead of being copy-pasted sixty different ways.

### Feature Block 1 — Catalog Engine
**Title:** Structure your catalog the way your business actually works
**Body:** Define attributes, families, and categories once. Every product inherits the right fields automatically, so a "T-Shirt" and a "Forklift" don't share a form — and nothing important gets skipped.

**Body:** Give suppliers a simple portal scoped to just their products. They fill in what you need; your team reviews and approves before anything goes live. No more email attachments.

**Title:** Publish everywhere from one click, not six exports

**Title:** Know what's broken before your customers do
**Body:** Kernle continuously scans your catalog for missing fields, inconsistent formatting, and thin descriptions — and tells you exactly what to fix, ranked by what matters most.

**Title:** Let AI do the first draft. Your team does the judgment call.

## 10. FAQ Section

**What problems does Kernle AI actually solve?**
Most teams don't have a "product data" tool — they have a spreadsheet, an ERP export, a shared drive of images, and a lot of manual copy-pasting between them. Kernle AI replaces that patchwork with a single system where product data is created once, enriched once, and distributed everywhere, so your team spends less time reconciling and more time launching.

**How does Kernle AI help with AI search and AI shopping assistants?**
AI tools can only recommend products they can accurately understand. Kernle structures your product data — attributes, specs, descriptions, and media — into a format that's readable by both people and machines, and scores each product on how well-prepared it is for AI-driven discovery. The better structured your data, the more likely AI tools are to surface and correctly describe your products.

**What kind of companies use Kernle AI?**
Kernle AI is built for teams managing catalogs that have outgrown spreadsheets — typically a few hundred SKUs and up — across retail, fashion, manufacturing, consumer goods, and B2B distribution. If you sell through more than one channel, or work with suppliers who send you product data in inconsistent formats, Kernle is built for you.

**Does Kernle AI replace our ERP or eCommerce platform?**
No — it sits between them. Kernle pulls raw product data in from ERPs, spreadsheets, or suppliers, and pushes clean, validated, enriched data out to your storefronts and marketplaces. It's the layer that makes sure everything downstream gets accurate, complete information.

**How does the AI enrichment actually work — does it just make things up?**
Kernle's AI suggestions are grounded in your own product data and images — it's drafting from what you already have, not inventing specs. Every suggestion shows a confidence level and requires a person to review and approve it before it's saved. Nothing publishes automatically.

**Can we try it before committing?**
Yes — you can start on a free plan with a limited number of SKUs to explore the core catalog, asset library, and AI enrichment tools before upgrading.

## Copy Notes for Whoever Implements This

- Anything in `[PLACEHOLDER]` brackets must be replaced with real data or removed — never launch with fabricated customer quotes, logos, or stats. That's a trust problem waiting to happen, not just a legal one.
- Keep the tone confident but not hypey — short sentences, concrete outcomes, minimal jargon. Avoid stacking superlatives ("revolutionary," "game-changing") — let the specificity do the persuading.
- Every AI-related claim on the page should describe something the product actually does by the time you ship Phase 6 — don't let marketing copy get ahead of the real feature set.
